Abstract of the Workshop
With AI starting to be the backbone of many processes across organizations, there is a need to simplify the access and the technologies which will enable them to reap the benefits of AI. Democratizing of AI tools across the entire organization is an imperative action.

In this talk, I will talk about the multiple ways this can be achieved and demo one example of such a platform and describe how the platform enables AI research�use�cases.
