Call for Posters

Posters at ICDDS 2026 welcome early results, demos, and emerging ideas.

The poster track is ideal for work that benefits from discussion, feedback, and exposure in a live conference setting.

Poster Session

Why submit a poster

The poster track provides a platform for authors to present preliminary work, practical demonstrations, and research-in-progress in data and decision systems.

Topics welcome

Poster themes

  • Probabilistic inference
  • Large language models
  • Data privacy
  • Federated learning
  • Optimization
  • Ethics in AI
  • Recommender systems
  • Urban computing
  • Foundational models
  • Responsible AI
  • Web analytics
  • System design
  • ML for embedded platforms
  • Cloud and stream computing
Submission guidelines

What to include

  • Poster submissions must be anonymized and follow double-blind review.
  • Maximum length is 3-4 pages in IEEE double-column format.
  • Submit as a PDF through Microsoft CMT.
  • Focus on clear visuals, key results, and concise explanations.
Poster format

Preparation tips

Prepare a printed poster in A0 portrait orientation. Highlight diagrams, figures, data flow, and results so the poster works as a conversation starter.
